# Original Hero Concept Prompt

- Use case: stylized-concept
- Asset type: production character turnaround for a rigged third-person WebGL game
- Primary request: Create an entirely original supernatural football-combat hero named Rook Vesper, the last human captain of Huntrix.
- Scene/backdrop: Flat neutral charcoal studio background with even lighting and no environmental props.
- Subject: One lean athletic warm-brown-skinned adult hero shown at identical scale in front, exact left profile, exact back, and three-quarter views. Neutral A-pose in the orthographic views. Dark swept-back five-clump hair with an undercut, angular original face, focused expression, small healed cut through the right eyebrow.
- Kit: Original black and dried-crimson fitted football-survival kit, broken high collar, cracked matte-carbon right shoulder guard, pale goal-net cord around the left forearm with restrained crimson runes, tapered shorts over leggings, short split sash behind the left hip, high asymmetric boots with silver toe cages and crimson heels. Original crescent-fang-and-kickoff-dot crest and fictional XI back glyph.
- Style/medium: Polished stylized 3D game-character concept render, low-poly production awareness, realistic PBR materials, clear construction seams and accessory attachment points.
- Composition/framing: Full body from head to soles in every view, consistent proportions and camera height, generous separation between views.
- Lighting/mood: Neutral modeling light; readable roughness and material breaks; no dramatic rim light.
- Color palette: #11131A, #8E1738, #E8335A, #C7CED8, #E2D8C5, #3B285F.
  Constraints: No real-player likeness, no recognizable club kit, no sponsor, no commercial logo, no copyrighted character, no cape, no weapon, no ball blocking the body, no extra limbs, no perspective distortion in the first three views, no watermark. Keep emissive areas below eight percent of the visible body.
