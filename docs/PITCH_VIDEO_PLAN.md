## What the player must record later

Record **one complete 10-minute run** at 1920×1080, 60 fps, fullscreen, with game audio. Two runs are safer but not mandatory. Do not narrate while playing.

During the run, try to capture:

1. The opening kickoff and a clean first attack.
2. A charged kick followed by recall through several enemies.
3. A curve, dash, Focus Kick and character ultimate.
4. Blood-shard collection and at least one upgrade choice.
5. Several visually distinct enemy types or an elite formation.
6. The goal-line defender blocking a shot and a successful goal.
7. Count Goalkeeper or the strongest boss encounter reached.
8. A dramatic high-density clear, goal or victory moment.

Leave menus and non-gameplay presentation footage to the editor. Keep playing naturally rather than attempting to perform a fixed script.

## Capture settings

- Use the frozen production build, not a development server.
- Record at 1920×1080 and 60 fps.
- Set Quality to **Quality** or **Balanced** if frame pacing suffers.
- Set the game FPS limit to 60 or 120.
- Disable the performance monitor.
- Keep SFX at 90–100% and music at 40–50%.
- Hide notifications and unrelated windows.
- Begin recording before selecting **Enter the Pitch** and stop only after the run ends.

## Editorial rules

- Let important mechanics play uninterrupted for 4–8 seconds so judges understand them.
- Retain kick, recall, upgrade, keeper, goal and ultimate sound effects.
- Duck music beneath narration instead of muting the game completely.
- Use only short on-screen labels: `AIM · CHARGE · KICK`, `CALL IT HOME`, `BUILD THE RUN`, `BREAK THE KEEPER`, and `EVERY KICKOFF STARTS A NEW STORY`.
- Use clean cuts, brief fades and impact cuts. Avoid template-heavy transitions.
- Keep the HUD visible during genuine gameplay.
- Add accurate subtitles for the complete narration.
- Export a narrated master and a narration-free backup.
