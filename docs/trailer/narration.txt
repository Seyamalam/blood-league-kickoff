One ball. One survivor. An entire blood league between you and the final whistle. This is Blood League: Kickoff.

We are Team Huntrix: Touhidul Alam Seyam and MD. Abtahee Kabir. We created a third-person football-combat roguelite where the stadium has fallen to vampires—and your football is the only weapon that can clear the pitch.

Movement, camera control and aiming work together across a full-sized field. Line up the shot, charge its power, add curve, and kick through enemy formations. But the ball does not magically return. Once it leaves your feet, you must survive without it, reposition, then call it home. Its return path is another attack, so every kick creates a decision: commit to the shot, chase the rebound, or recall through the crowd for maximum damage. Dash, tackle, Focus Kick and character ultimates turn familiar football actions into fast, readable combat.

Defeated enemies drop blood shards that level your striker during the match. Every draft can reshape the run with active weapons, passive boosts, curses and powerful evolutions. Build for raw shot damage, wider pickup range, greater health, life steal, rapid recall, orbiting footballs or devastating set pieces. Six heroes begin with different traits and signature ultimates, so the same kickoff can lead to a completely different strategy.

This is not football scenery wrapped around a survivor game. The goal matters. A dedicated goal-line defender reads your attacks, Count Goalkeeper protects the final objective, and scoring requires timing, positioning and control of the ball while the enemy wave keeps advancing. A saved shot can become a dangerous counterattack. A clean opening can change the entire match.

Eleven enemy roles create distinct formations, supported by elites, minibosses and multi-phase encounters. Five supernatural stadiums change the atmosphere with unique architecture, weather, crowds and Blood Moon transformations. Outside each run, Career mode tracks fixtures and championship progress. Players can unlock content, chase mastery and challenges, compare local leaderboard builds, choose a hero, or create and save their own block-style footballer.

For us, Kickoff means more than restarting a football match. It is the first action that begins a journey. Every run starts with that single kick, which launches the combat, the build, the rising enemy pressure and the fight toward the final goal. One simple beginning sets an entire chain of events into motion.

Kick hard. Call it home. Survive the final whistle. Blood League: Kickoff, created by Team Huntrix.
