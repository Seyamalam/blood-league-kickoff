# **GameJam Rulebook**

**Powered by**

**Overview & Purpose** 

This game jam is designed to push your creative boundaries and challenge you to develop a polished game within a limited timeframe. This rulebook outlines everything you need to know to participate, from the theme reveal to submission guidelines and judging criteria. So, grab your favorite development tools, gather your team, and prepare to unleash your creativity\!

**How this works** 

- **Online Round:** The full theme will be revealed at the start of the online round. Your team will have a designated time frame to design, develop, and submit a complete game based on this theme.   
- **Onsite Round:** All online submissions will be evaluated to select the Top **15** Teams. If selected, you will advance to the onsite grand finale to present the game you created during the online round. No modifications are allowed to your game after the online submission. 


**Rounds to Face** 

- **Online** 

The full theme will be revealed at the official start of the jam. Participants will develop their games from scratch and submit the following components before the deadline: 

- **A fully working build** of the game on [itch.io](http://itch.io) (Windows or Web).

- **A gameplay video submission** explaining your game's mechanics, design, and connection to the theme.

- **A link to your public GitHub repository** to verify development history and ensure project integrity.

- **Onsite** 

The onsite round will take place live at the **Islamic University of Technology (IUT), Boardbazar, Gazipur**. 

- ​ This is a presentation-only round for the Top **15** selected teams. You will pitch and  	demo the exact game submitted during the online round to the judges.

- The preliminary scores given by the organizers to select the top 15 **will not carry** over to the onsite round, other than the scores for video (pitch) submission.

**Event Timeline** 

* Teams can register from **17th June** to the end of the online round.  
* The online round will run for **7 days**, with an approximate date of **July 14 \- July 20, 2026\.**   
* After the online round concludes, participants will be required to submit a pitch video presenting their game by **July 21, 2026\.**. This video should clearly explain the core gameplay mechanics, as well as how the game connects to and interprets the theme.  
* The onsite round will be held at Islamic University of Technology on **July 25, 2026\.**

**Note:** Once the online submission deadline passes, your project files and GitHub repository must freeze. **Any post-deadline modifications before the onsite event will result in immediate disqualification.**  
**Registration and Team Formation** 

* Each team will consist of **1 to 3** members, once formed, team members cannot be substituted for the remainder of the competition.  
* Cross-university teams are permitted.

* Eligible participants include higher secondary students, undergraduate students, and graduate students of HSC-21 or equivalent.  
* Registration for the online stage is **free**, while the onsite round will require a registration fee of **700 BDT per team** (only for selected teams)

**Rules and Guidelines** 

* Teams must have a unique name.

* Participants will open an empty git repository and start developing from there.

* Participants will be able to use any engine or framework as long as the end user doesn't need to install additional software.  
* Participants will create a new game project. Which means you can not edit any previous game project files for this game jam.  
* Participants are allowed to reuse assets.  
* Restrictions for using assets are:

  * No NSFW content (visuals, audio, text, etc.)  
  * No assets that goes against the terms of services of [itch.io](http://itch.io)  
  * No copyrighted assets

* Online Round:

  * Participants will submit their game within the deadline with proper information.

  * Participants must submit a short pitch video explaining their game. It should be submitted within one day after the online round ends.  
  * Participants will maintain a git repository of their game.

* Onsite Round:

  * Participants will bring their project and game executable files.  
  * Participants will bring their student ID card that was used for registration

  * The participants will bring their devices (laptop, mouse, keyboard, etc.).  
* Game submission:

  * The game will be submitted to the game jam event on itch.io.

  * One team can submit only one game.

  * Participants will submit builds for windows or web.

  * Participants can upload multiple builds for different platforms, but have to be submitted before the deadline.

  * Participants will be able to make changes to their submission before the deadline.  
  * Participants can update information about the game after submission in the game page on itch.io.

  * Participants will include the following information in their submission:

    * Team name

    * Name of the team members

    * Used engine or frameworks

    * How to install (if needed)

    * How to play

    * Git repository link

    * Video link (YouTube)

    * Anything else that participants might think it’s important to know or add (such as asset credits or game breaking bug)  
* Video submission:

  * Participants will create a short video of gameplay and explain it.

  * Participants will upload the video on youtube and provide the link.

  * The video will be used to showcase it on social media.

  * The video name will follow this format, “TeamName\_GameName”

**NOTE:** The authority has the prerogative to make conclusive decisions at any point during the entire competition duration.

**Judging Criteria**    

The games will be judged based on 5 criteria :

| Criteria | Description | Weight |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Theme | How well the theme was adopted | 25% |
| Gameplay | How interesting the gameplay features are | 25% |
| Design | How well the game is structured and balanced as an experience​ | 25% |
| Visual & Audio | How well the game looks and sounds | 15% |
| Video (Pitch) Submission | How well the video explains the game mechanics, features and theme adaptation | 10% |
| **Total** |  | **100%** |

**Evaluation** 

- Online Round Evaluation 

  * Submitted prototypes will be played and scored based on:

    * Whether the prototype is working properly

    * Alignment with the theme

    * Innovativeness of the idea

  * All submissions will be ranked based on respective scores

  * Late submissions will be disqualified

  * Cross checking will be carried out against video submissions.

  * These scores won’t carry forward to onsite round except for scores 

    on pitch submission.


- Onsite Round Evaluation 

  The judges will be looking for games that excel in the following areas:

* **Game Design:** Is the core gameplay loop engaging and fun? Does it offer a satisfying challenge?  
* **Game Mechanics:** How well do the mechanics support the theme and overall gameplay experience?  
* **Theme Adaptation:** This is a key focus\! Did you successfully incorporate the full theme, including the newly revealed element, into your existing

  prototype? Is the adaptation creative and well-integrated?

* **Aesthetics:** Do the visual style and soundscape complement the game's theme and gameplay?  
* **Additional Judge-Discretionary Criteria:** The judges may consider other aspects like innovation, technical polish, and overall polish.
